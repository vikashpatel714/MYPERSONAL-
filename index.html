About

Vikash Patel is an Assistant Professor and researcher in Computer Science and Engineering. He holds an M.Tech. in Artificial Intelligence and Robotics and a B.Tech. in Computer Science and Engineering from Gautam Buddha University, Greater Noida. His research and teaching interests include Quantum Computing, Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and emerging computing technologies. He has contributed to IEEE conference publications and is passionate about developing accessible, practical learning resources for students and researchers.
