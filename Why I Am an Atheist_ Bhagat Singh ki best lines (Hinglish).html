Why I Am
an Atheist

Lahore jail ki ek cell mein likha gaya essay, apni phaansi se kuch mahine pehle. Is page par uski 7 sabse mazboot baatein hain, aasaan Hinglish aur real-life examples ke saath.

Bhagat Singh, 1930  |  Page by Vikash Patel

Lines se pehle: essay ke peeche ki kahani

Ye essay kisi shaant library mein nahi likha gaya. Ise ek 23 saal ke krantikari ne likha, jise pata tha ki court use shayad maut ki saza dega. Ye timeline jaanne ke baad neeche ki har line zyada gehri lagti hai.

  • 28 Sept 1907. Bhagat Singh ka janm Punjab ke ek gaon mein hua, ek aise parivar mein jo azaadi ke andolan se jura tha.
  • 13 April 1919. Amritsar mein Jallianwala Bagh narsanhaar. Kaha jaata hai ki bachpan mein wo baad mein us jagah gaye the. Aisi ghatnaon ne dukh aur takleef ke baare mein unke sawaal gehre kiye.
  • 1928 se 1929. Saunders case aur Assembly mein bomb (8 April 1929). Unhone khud giraftaari di, taaki court ko apna manch bana saken.
  • June se Oct 1929. Jail mein political kaidiyon ko barabar haq dilane ke liye hunger strike. Ye strike 116 din chali.
  • Oct 1930. Lahore Central Jail mein unhone ye essay likha. Ek saathi kaidi, dharmik Sardar Randhir Singh, aur kuch aur logon ka kehna tha ki wo ghamand (vanity) ki wajah se nastik ban gaye. Ye essay usi ka jawab hai.
  • 23 March 1931. Rajguru aur Sukhdev ke saath unhe phaansi di gayi. Umar: 23 saal.
  • 27 Sept 1931. Unki maut ke baad ye essay Lahore ke akhbaar The People mein chhapa.

Line 1 of 7  |  Sawaal poochhne par

Any man who stands for progress has to criticize, disbelieve and challenge every item of the old faith.

Iska matlab

Koi baat sirf isliye mat maano ki wo purani hai, mashhoor hai ya bade-buzurgon ne kahi hai. Use parkho. Agar wo test mein khari utre toh rakho, nahi toh badlo. Unke hisaab se tarakki ki yahi keemat hai.

Real-life example

Raja Ram Mohan Roy ne sati pratha ko challenge kiya, jise sadiyon se "parampara" kehkar bachaya ja raha tha. Aaj agar koi student poochhta hai "ye rule kyun hai?" aur use sahi jawab milta hai, toh wo chhoti scale par wahi kaam kar raha hai.

Khud try karo

Ek aisi baat chuno jo tumne virasat mein payi hai (career, shaadi, success ke baare mein). Poochho: ye kisne kaha, aur saboot kya hai? Use reject karna zaroori nahi hai. Use parakhna zaroori hai.

Line 2 of 7  |  Khud sochne par

Criticism and independent thinking are the two necessary traits of a revolutionary.

Iska matlab

Krantikari wo nahi jo sabse zor se chillaye. Krantikari wo hai jo vicharon ko khud tole aur bheed se bhi asehmat hone ki himmat rakhe.

Real-life example

Galileo ko Church ne majboor kiya ki wo apni baat wapas le, ki Prithvi ghoomti hai. Sadiyon baad, jo sirf maani hui baat dohrata raha wo bhula diya gaya, aur jisne telescope se khud dekha wo yaad rakha gaya.

Aaj ki zindagi mein

Bina check kiye viral message forward karna independent thinking ka ulta hai. Aur kisi answer ko bina samjhe ratta maarna bhi.

Line 3 of 7  |  Ghamand ke ilzaam par

Ghamand ya sachchi soch? Wo is ilzaam ka jawab gusse se nahi, tarkon se dete hain.

Iska matlab

Kuch logon ne kaha ki wo bhagwan ko isliye nahi maante kyunki unhe apne aap par ghamand hai. Wo kehte hain ki ye ek vyaktigat hamla hai, tark nahi. Phir wo apni asli soch ek-ek kadam karke samjhate hain. (Ye hamare shabdon mein saar hai, seedha quote nahi.)

Real-life example

Jab kisi ki alag raay par kaha jaata hai "tum sirf attention ke liye bol rahe ho", toh raay par baat hi nahi hoti. Debate mein ise kehte hain baat ke bajaye insaan par hamla karna.

Ye kyun zaroori hai

Achhi aadat: jab kisi se asehmat ho, toh jo usne kaha uska jawab do, ye mat socho ki uska irada kya hoga.

Line 4 of 7  |  Apne pairon par khade hone par

It is not child's play to stand upon one's own legs in the tempests and storms.

Iska matlab

Wo imaandari se maante hain ki bhagwan par vishwas mushkil waqt mein sahara deta hai, aur us sahare ke bina jeena kathin hai. Unke liye nastikta aasaan raasta nahi thi. Iska matlab tha apni taakat aur himmat par bharosa karna.

Real-life example

Do students ka mushkil exam hai. Ek taveez rakhkar kismat ka intezaar karta hai. Dusra zimmedari leta hai: revise karta hai, kamzor topics theek karta hai, result accept karta hai. Bhagat Singh ne doosra attitude chuna, wo bhi tab jab sar par phaansi ki saza thi.

Unki fairness dekho

Wo believers ka mazaak nahi udate. Wo maante hain ki vishwas logon ko sukoon de sakta hai. Unki baat bas itni hai ki wo zindagi ka saamna udhaar ke sahare ke bina karna chahte the.

Line 5 of 7  |  Duniya ke dukh par

Why did he create this world of woes and miseries, a veritable, eternal hell of unending unrest?

Iska matlab

Ye purana "problem of evil" hai. Agar bhagwan sarvashaktimaan aur poori tarah achha hai, toh akaal, narsanhaar aur garibi kyun hain? Wo kehte hain ki aam jawab (kismat, pichhle janam ke karm, bhagwan ki yojana) unhe santusht nahi karte.

Real-life example

Jallianwala Bagh, 1919: ek band baagh mein phanse nihatthe parivaron par goliyan chalin. Baad mein 1943 ke Bengal akaal ne lakhon jaanein li. Aise hadson ke baad aam log bhi wahi sawaal poochhte hain: kyun?

Doosra paksh, balance ke liye

Believers ne sadiyon mein kai jawab diye hain: ki dukh free will, vikaas ya karuna ke liye jagah banata hai, ya ki insaan poori tasveer nahi dekh sakta. Ye page unka tark dikhata hai. Kya convincing lagta hai, ye tum tay karo.

Line 6 of 7  |  Matlab ke liye prarthna par

I consider it to be an act of degradation and demoralisation. For such petty selfish motives, I shall never pray.

Iska matlab

Ek dost ne kaha ki aakhri din aayenge toh tum prarthna karne lagoge. Unhone jawab diya ki sirf darr ya nijee fayde ke liye prarthna karna insaan ki garima girata hai. Wo apni jaan bachane ke liye bhagwan se sauda nahi karenge.

Real-life example

"Bhagwan, 90 percent aa gaye toh har hafte mandir aaunga." Ye sauda hai, bhakti nahi. Unka aitraaz isi sauda-wali soch par tha, jahan vishwas ko darr aur ehsaan maangne ka tool bana liya jaata hai.

Itihaas ka wazan

Jab unke pitaji ne tribunal se unki jaan bachane ki arzi di, toh Bhagat Singh ne unhe virodh mein patra likha. Wo apne usoolon ki keemat par bachna nahi chahte the.

Line 7 of 7  |  Ant ka saamna karne par

Let us see how steadfast I am.

Iska matlab

Unhe chunauti di gayi thi ki aakhir mein wo toot jayenge aur prarthna karenge. Wo khud se vaada karte hain ki wo apne vishwas par tike rahenge aur maut ka saamna shaant man se karenge.

Real-life example

Pressure mein imaandari socho: ek employee jo galat report par sign karne se mana kar de, ya wo student jo tab bhi nakal nahi karta jab sab kar rahe hon. Kisi vishwas ka asli test tab hota hai jab use nibhane ki keemat chukani padti hai.

Phir kya hua

23 March 1931 ki shaam ke bayan unhe shaant aur padhte hue batate hain. Essay ki aakhri chunauti ka jawab unhone apni zindagi se diya.

Har cheez par sawaal uthao. Khud socho. Imaandar raho, chahe keemat kitni bhi ho.

Essay ka dil yahi hai, chahe tum bhagwan ke baare mein uske nishkarsh se sehmat ho ya nahi.
